Trace

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 5, 2026 · Last updated: November 5, 2026

Trace works without an account. Your memories and photos are kept on your iPhone, and nothing is uploaded unless you choose to — by turning on the optional cloud backup or by sharing something yourself.

1. Overview

Trace ("we", "the app") is a personal memory and life-milestone app for iOS. We are committed to your privacy. This policy explains what data the app handles, how it is used, and when any of it leaves your device.

2. Data stored on your device

All of the following is stored on your iPhone. None of it is sent to our servers unless you turn on the optional cloud backup (which copies your memories and photos, but never your home area — see "Cloud backup (Premium)" below):

  • Memory records (title, date, notes, location coordinates, place name, country)
  • Photos you add to memories (stored in the app's private sandbox)
  • Your "home" — an estimate of the area where you live (a center coordinate, the names of a few places nearby, and a rough radius), worked out on your device from the locations and dates of your photos, or set by you in Settings. It is used only to group your photos into trips, is stored only on your device, and is not included in backup exports.
  • App settings (language preference, theme selection, notification preferences)
  • Widget data (your most recent memory, displayed on your Home Screen)

An account is optional: the app works completely without one, and signing in does not upload any of the items listed above. We do not have the ability to access your memories unless you choose to turn on a cloud feature that stores them (see "Optional account" below).

3. Third-party services

The app uses the following third-party services for specific features. Each is optional or limited in scope:

Mapbox (Maps & Location Search)

The app uses Mapbox for the 2D map view and place search. Mapbox's privacy policy is available at mapbox.com/legal/privacy.

  • When you search for a place by name, the text you type in the search field is sent to Mapbox's Geocoding API.
  • To turn a coordinate into a place name, the app sends that single coordinate to Mapbox's Geocoding API and receives a city and country name back. This happens when you use "Current Location" while adding or editing a memory, when you place or adjust the pin on the map while adding a memory or changing the location of a saved memory, when the app reads a GPS coordinate already embedded in a photo's EXIF data to suggest a location while you're adding a memory (including when you start from a photo instead of picking a place first), when "Import from Gallery" names a group of photos (one coordinate per group), and when "Import from Gallery" works out the name of your home area (the coordinate of its center and of a few frequently visited places nearby), and when a memory you saved while offline still has no place name — the next few times you open the app, it quietly tries once more to look up the name for the coordinate already stored with that memory, and stops trying after a few attempts. In every case, only that single coordinate is sent — the photo itself and every other piece of its metadata (other EXIF fields, filename, image data, etc.) stay on your device and are never sent anywhere.
  • When you view the 2D map, map tiles are loaded from Mapbox's servers. Like any web request, this involves your IP address and the map area being viewed. Previously viewed areas are cached on your device for offline use.
  • We have disabled Mapbox's optional usage telemetry in the app.
  • If you use "Current Location", your position is determined by your device's on-board GPS. We do not store it or send it to any server of ours; it is sent to Mapbox only as the single coordinate described above, to look up the place name.

GitHub Pages (Feature Configuration)

When it starts, the app may download a small public configuration file hosted on GitHub Pages. We use it to switch certain display features off, for everyone or in specific regions, when a law or regulation requires it. GitHub's privacy statement is available at docs.github.com.

  • The request contains no personal data, no identifier, and nothing about your memories. Like any web request, it involves your IP address.
  • The file is the same for every user. We do not use this request for analytics or tracking.
  • If the file cannot be downloaded, for example when you are offline, the app keeps working as before.

Anthropic Claude (AI Title Suggestions — Optional)

When you choose to use AI-generated title suggestions while creating a memory, the app sends only the following text to Anthropic's API:

  • The place name, city, and country of the memory
  • The date of the memory

Your photos and notes are never sent to Anthropic. This feature only runs when you request suggestions — if you do not use it, nothing is sent. Anthropic's privacy policy is available at anthropic.com/privacy.

Apple (StoreKit / In-App Purchases)

If you purchase optional themes (Globe Explorer Pack, Tech Pack, or Special Editions), the transaction is processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive confirmation that a purchase was completed but do not receive any payment or personal information. Apple's privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Trace Premium (subscription). If you subscribe to Trace Premium, Apple processes the payment and we never receive your payment details. To switch Premium on for your account, the app sends the signed subscription transaction that Apple issues to our service, and Apple also sends us notifications when your subscription renews, lapses or is refunded.

  • What we receive and keep: the transaction identifiers Apple assigns to your subscription, its current status (active, lapsed or refunded) and whether it will renew, the product, the time of the latest status change, and an opaque account token that links the subscription to your Trace account. This is linked to your account.
  • Why: only to know whether your account has Premium, so the features it covers (currently cloud backup) turn on and off correctly. We do not use it for advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • Who processes it: Apple, and our account service on Cloudflare, which stores the data above on our behalf.
  • How long: for as long as your account exists; deleting your account deletes it. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription — manage that in your Apple Account settings.

Optional account (Sign in with Apple / Sign in with Google)

You can optionally sign in with Apple or Google so that you can protect your memories with features that need an account. You never need an account to create, view, edit, export or delete memories on your device.

  • What we collect when you sign in: your Apple or Google account identifier (an opaque ID issued by Apple or Google), the email address on that account (if you choose "Hide My Email" with Apple, this is Apple's private relay address), your name (only if Apple or Google provides it, and Apple provides it only the first time), and a session token that keeps you signed in. The session token is stored in your iPhone's Keychain, on that device only.
  • What signing in does not send: signing in shares only your Apple or Google identity. No memories, photos, places, or other content from the app are uploaded as part of signing in.
  • Why we use it: only to recognise your account and to provide the account-based features you turn on. We do not use it for advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • Who processes it: our account service runs on Cloudflare, which stores the data above on our behalf. Apple and Google process the sign-in itself under their own privacy policies (apple.com/legal/privacy, policies.google.com/privacy).
  • How long we keep it: for as long as your account exists. Signing out removes the session from your device and ends it on our service. Deleting your account removes it entirely (see below).
  • Deleting your account: you can delete your account at any time in the app (Settings, then your account). This permanently deletes your account record, the sign-in identities linked to it, and any cloud data stored under it, and revokes the app's access to your Apple or Google account (for Apple, the sign-in token we hold is revoked with Apple). Deleting your account does not delete anything stored on your device, and does not cancel an App Store subscription, which you manage in your Apple Account settings.
  • Memories and photos stay on your device unless you yourself turn on the optional cloud backup, which is described in the next section.

Cloud backup (Premium)

Cloud backup is optional and is part of Trace Premium. It is off until you turn it on, and turning it on is a deliberate step: the app shows you what is backed up before anything leaves your phone. You never need it to use Trace.

  • What is backed up: the memories on your device, including their photos, videos, Live Photo motion clips, voice notes, places (including precise coordinates), dates, notes and trips, and the thumbnails and preview images the app made from your photos. Your original photos in the iOS Photos library are not uploaded.
  • What is never backed up: the estimate of your home area always stays on your device and is not uploaded.
  • Encryption: the app encrypts your content on your iPhone before it is uploaded. The encryption keys are managed by our service, not held only by you, so this is not end-to-end encryption: we operate the service that can decrypt the copy in order to restore it to you, and we do not look at your content.
  • Who processes it: the encrypted copy is stored with Cloudflare (Cloudflare R2), and the records that index it are kept in our Cloudflare account database. This content is linked to your account.
  • Why: only to keep a copy for you and to restore it when you ask. We do not use it for advertising, analytics, tracking or to train models, and we do not share it with other people.
  • Turning it off or deleting it: you can turn cloud backup off at any time in Settings. When you do, you choose whether to keep or to delete the copy in the cloud. Deleting the cloud copy removes the backed-up memories and files from our service and does not affect anything on your device. Deleting a memory on a phone that has backup turned on also removes it from the cloud copy.
  • If your subscription ends: backing up stops, and your existing cloud copy is kept for 12 months so that you can still restore it; after that it may be deleted. You can delete it yourself at any time before then.
  • If you delete your account: the cloud copy is deleted together with your account.
  • Availability: cloud backup is not offered in mainland China. Anyone who already has a backup can still turn it off and delete it.

4. Device permissions

The app requests the following iOS permissions only when needed for specific features:

  • Photos: To let you add photos to your memories. Photos are copied into the app's private storage — your original Photos library is not modified. When you add a photo while creating a memory, the app also reads that photo's embedded GPS coordinate (if it has one) and its capture date, entirely on your device, to suggest a location and date for the memory — this is what lets you start a memory from a single photo without picking a place first. If a location is suggested this way, that one coordinate is sent to Mapbox to look up a place name (see "Mapbox" above); the photo itself is never sent. If you use the optional "Import from Gallery" feature, the app scans your photo library's location and date metadata on your device to suggest memories from past trips, to group them into trips, and to work out roughly where you live so that everyday photos taken near home are not mistaken for trips. The scan itself happens entirely on-device and your photos are never uploaded. The app may also run this same on-device check automatically each time you open it, to suggest a memory from photos you haven't saved yet — nothing about this automatic check is transmitted anywhere. To name each group of photos and your home area, single coordinates (one per group, plus the center of your home area and a few places near it) are sent to Mapbox (see "Mapbox" above). Where your home is stays on your device and is not included in backup exports.
  • Location (When In Use): To automatically detect your current location when adding a memory. Used only on request — the app does not track your location in the background. The one exception is the place-name lookup described under "Mapbox" above: if a memory was saved offline without a place name, opening the app may re-send that memory's already-stored coordinate to look up its name. That is a lookup for a coordinate you already saved — the app does not read your GPS or determine your position in the background.
  • Notifications: To deliver "On This Day" reminders about memories from past years. These notifications are scheduled locally on your device and do not require a server.

5. Data we do not collect

  • We do not collect analytics or crash reports.
  • We do not display advertising.
  • We do not require an account. If you choose to sign in, we collect only the account data described under "Optional account" above.
  • We do not share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.
  • We do not use tracking technologies (cookies, device fingerprinting, etc.).

6. Backup, export, and sharing

The app includes a local backup feature that lets you export all your memories and photos as a ZIP file. This file is created on your device and shared using iOS's standard share sheet (AirDrop, iCloud Drive, etc.). We do not have access to these ZIP backup files — this paragraph is about that local export; the optional cloud backup is described under "Cloud backup (Premium)" above. The ZIP backup contains your memories and photos; it does not include the estimate of your home area, because that is the most sensitive location the app derives and backup files are often shared.

When you choose to share content from the app — such as a globe video — it is handed directly to the destination you select (the iOS share sheet, or an app like Instagram). What happens to shared content after that is governed by the destination service's own privacy policy.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device for as long as you keep it. You can delete any individual memory at any time from within the app, or delete all of them at once from Settings — this removes every memory and trip, the photo and video copies the app made, time-message voice recordings, the widget's cached photo copies, and the app's estimate of your home area. Your original photos in the iOS photo library are never touched. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. Unless you turn on the optional cloud backup, we never receive your memories or photos, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our side for those; if you do turn it on, the copy in the cloud is kept until you delete it, turn off backup and choose to delete it, or delete your account, and — after a subscription ends — for the period described under "Cloud backup (Premium)". If you created an optional account, the account data described above is kept until you delete your account in the app, which removes it from our service.

8. Your rights

Privacy regulations such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export, and delete your personal data. By default all of your data lives on your own device, so you can exercise every one of these rights directly in the app — viewing, editing, exporting (via backup), and deleting your data, one memory at a time or all at once from Settings — without needing to make a request to us. If you turn on the optional cloud backup, you can also delete the copy in the cloud yourself in the app (Settings, then Cloud Backup), or by deleting your account; if you need help with anything we hold, contact us.

9. Security

Your data is protected by iOS's built-in security: the app's storage is sandboxed from other apps, and is encrypted by your device's hardware encryption whenever your iPhone is locked. We recommend using a device passcode to keep this protection effective. If you turn on cloud backup, your content is encrypted on your device before upload and stored encrypted with Cloudflare; the keys are managed by our service (see "Cloud backup (Premium)").

10. Children's privacy

Trace is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.
